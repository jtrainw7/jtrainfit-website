📖 The Word
Iron sharpens iron.
As iron sharpens iron, so one person sharpens another. This verse is where we got our name — and it's where we're starting.
"As iron sharpens iron, so one person sharpens another."
Proverbs 27:17

Here's what most men miss: the sharpening doesn't happen in isolation. Iron doesn't sharpen itself. You need friction. You need contact. You need another piece of iron pressing against you.

That's what this community is. It's not a motivational post you scroll past on a Tuesday morning. It's a commitment — to press in, to show up, to be the kind of man who makes the men around him sharper just by being in the room.

This week, ask yourself honestly: Who is sharpening me right now? And — equally important — who am I sharpening? If you can't answer both questions, that's your work this week.

The Forged Foundation
Full body calisthenics. No equipment. No gym. Just your body, your will, and 30 minutes.
